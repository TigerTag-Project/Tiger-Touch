## The board component

`firmware/components/PandaTouch_IDF/` is BigTreeTech's component for the Panda
Touch (PandaTouch_IDF, published by BIGTREETECH), **patched here**: a
run-time option turns the picture, and the touch coordinates with it, by 180 degrees,
and upright LVGL draws straight into the panel's frame buffer (direct mode).

**To confirm.** Its README says it is "provided under the MIT License (assumed)",
and neither the vendored copy nor the upstream repository ships a `LICENSE` file
(checked 2026-10-13). It is redistributed here on that stated intent, with its
README. Asking BigTreeTech to state it is open (`docs/ROADMAP.md`); if they
state otherwise, it is replaced by a reference to the upstream repository plus a patch.

## Brand artwork

The printer-maker logos in `firmware/main/brand_logos.c` and the boot picture
belong to their owners and to the TigerTag project; see
[TRADEMARK.md](TRADEMARK.md). The logos are small monochrome renderings used only
to say which brand a printer is.

The pictograms for the nozzle, the bed and the chamber (`firmware/tools/pictos/`)
are Tiger Studio Manager's own; the clock is Studio's too, and the file and the
flag are drawn in the same style.

The pictures of the screen - `assets/tiger-touch-device.png`,
`assets/tiger-touch-splash.png`, everything in `assets/devices/` (the animated
`tiger-touch-views.webp` included) and the installer's `device*.png` - are
BigTreeTech's line drawing of the Panda Touch from its board component's
documentation (`docs/images/pandatouch.png`, MIT as stated by that component, see
above), its lines turned grey and a Tiger Touch screen set into it. The
`BIGTREETECH` mark on them is theirs.

The README's other pictures come from the TigerTag project's own Tiger Studio
Manager:

- `assets/printers/lineup.png` puts together the printer pictures Tiger Studio
  shows for each model; the printers and their pictures belong to their makers
  and are shown only to say which printers Tiger Touch works with.
- `assets/brands/brands.svg` gathers Tiger Studio's brand marks, greyed. The
  marks belong to their owners ([TRADEMARK.md](TRADEMARK.md)); the Prusa,
  Klipper and OctoPrint ones are plain letter or outline marks, not those
  projects' official logos.
- `assets/ecosystem/` holds the TigerSystem products' own pictures.
